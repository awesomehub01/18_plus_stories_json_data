Mere bachpan hi mein meri mangni mere chacha zaad bhaai se kar di gai thi yeh mere dada jani ki khawaish thi aur mere aboo aur chacha dono apne walid ki baat ko taal nah sakte thay. hamaare ghar mein har treh ki ashaaish ki frawani thi. dada jani 4 marboon ke maalek thay. un ke bare baite yehni mer Aboo Dilawar khan arhat karte thay aur chacha Bahdur Khan zameeino ki dekh bhaal karte. Ghar mein har trah ki gunjaiesh aur ashaaesh ki frawani hone ke bawajood mahol strict tha. mein ne high school ke baad college mein jana chaha tu dada jani ne manah kar diya. keh baitiun ke liye itni taleem kaafi hai. mere armaano par aos par gai aur ghar mein ami aur chachi ka hath bitaane lagi. jiss se mein ghar grahsti mein taiz ho gai aaur har qism ke khane pakane mein maher ho gai. mere mangaitar Umair mujh se 2 saal bara tha aur college mein parhta aur hostel mein rehta tha. mere 2 bhai Kamaal khan aur jamal khan jo keh mujh se bare thay woh bhi city mein ussi college aur hostel mein rehte thay. ab dada jani ko meri shadi ki fikar lag gai magar mere mangaitar ka kheyal tha taleem mukamal kar k shadi kare. iss treh us ne apne waldain ke zareah 4 saal ka time le iya aur apni parhaai mein masroof hogeya. magar honi ko kaun taale sakta hai 2 saal hi guzre thay keh dada jani ka inteqaal ho geya.. Bad qismati se dono bhaiun yehni mere waled aur chacha mein jaeidaad ki taqseem mein aik doosre se ikhtelaaf paida hoye aur 2 salon ke andar andar baat itni barhi keh makaanon ke durmiyaan dewar utha di gai aur bol chaal tak band kar di gai. us waqt mein 22 sal ki ho chuki thi. mere waldain ko umeed thi keh kuch bhi ho jaay dada jani ki ki hoi baat nibhaai jaeigi. issi liye gharon mein baat cheet nah hone ke bawajood mein umair ki magaitar hi thi. chacha logon ka kiya irada tha kuch kaha nahein ja sakta tha. mere waled saheb sakht tbiyat ke magar dil ke narm hein aur chacha jaan tu ziddi mashoor hein. khair umair ne jab apni taleem mukmal ki tu us waqt mein 24 saal ki ho chuki thi. umair university se taleem mkml kr ke wapis aaya tu mere waldein ko umeed thi keh ab un ki tarf se shadi ke liye kaha jaaeiga aur mere waldain ne tiyari bhi shuro kar di thi. magar aik din ham ne suna keh umair ki tu agle mah apni phoophi ki baiti se shadi ho rahi hai. tu hum logon ne chup saadh li, mere waled ne kaha keh meri bhanji bhi meri baiti hi hai. un se kiya gila karein. ab mere waldein ne bhi mera rishta dhoondna shuroo kar diya. kiunkeh woh chahte thay umair ki shadi se pehle nah ho tu kuch din baad ho jaay. Abba ki arhat mein aik aur arhti tha jo keh bahut shareef aur accha karobaari tha. un se se aboo ne baat ki uss ke do baite thay. aik tu randwa tha aur uski aik baiti thi woh khud 39 saal ka tha. uska rishta woh shareef admi jin ka naam Sher Bahadur hai le kar aaya tu usse mere waldein ne inkaar kar diya. aik tu umron mein bahut ziyada farq aur phir uski aik baiti bhi thi, jis ki wjeh se unhon ne inkaar kar diya. magar unki wife ko mein bahut pasand aai thi tu aik hafta baad woh apne chote baite ka rishta le kar aay jo keh 27 saal ka tha aur Dubai mein hota tha. mere waldein ne un se sochne ka time liya. kiunkeh un logon ki raahesh city se baher gaaon mein thi. un ke baare maaloomat hasel karte 2 hafta lag gaye. Un ke khandan ki reputation acchi thi. 2 murabah zameen ke ilawa arhat mein karobaar. aik hi baiti sameera jo biyahi ja chuki thi. bare baite Sameer ki shadi ke baad aik baiti Areeba hoi 3 saal pehle uski maan yehni Sameer ki waife faut ho gi. ab Areeba 8 saal ki hai aur chota baita sagheer 27 saal Dubai mein mulazim hai. mere waldain ki nazron mein khandan accha aur khata peeta tha. unhon ne haan kar di aur 2 maah baad meri Sagheer hassan se shadi hogai. aur 2 maah ke baad woh wapis Dubai apni job par chale gaye....
